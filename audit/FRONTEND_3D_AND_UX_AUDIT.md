## 2. 3D Anatomy & Medical Visualization Deep-Dive

### 2.1 3D Mesh Geometry & Asset Provenance
* **Mesh Asset:** `frontend/public/models/heart.glb` (File size: 2.24 MB).
* **Asset Origin:** Programmatically generated by `frontend/scripts/generate_heart_model.mjs` using Three.js mathematical curves and procedural geometries (`TubeGeometry`, `CatmullRomCurve3D`, `LatheGeometry`).
* **Geometry Quality:**
  - Ventricular myocardium and atria are modeled as organic solids.
  - Great vessels (Aorta, Pulmonary Artery, SVC/IVC) feature hollow interior lumens visible in X-ray mode.
  - Epicaridal fat pads are positioned along the sulci.
  - Coronary arteries are smooth tubular extrusions traversing the epicardial surface.
* **Anatomical Mapping & Vessel Courses:**
  - **LAD (Left Anterior Descending):** Accurately follows the anterior interventricular sulcus down to the apex. Focus camera preset: `Anterior`.
  - **LCX (Left Circumflex):** Traverses the left atrioventricular groove toward the posterolateral myocardial base. Focus camera preset: `Lateral`.
  - **RCA (Right Coronary Artery):** Courses along the right atrioventricular sulcus toward the crux of the heart. Focus camera preset: `Right AV / Inferior`.

### 2.2 Material Shaders, PBR Rendering & Lighting
* **PBR Shaders:**
  - `MeshStandardMaterial` configured with procedural muscle fiber normal maps (`createMuscleFibersTexture`) to give striated myocardial texture.
  - Aorta and pulmonary arteries feature low roughness (0.24) and subtle specular sheen.
  - Coronary vessels utilize emissive lighting with dynamic intensity: resting state (0.10), hovered state (0.28), and active selected highlight (0.45).
* **Lighting Rig:** Three-point studio lighting with directional key light, fill light, and subtle ambient light. Avoids harsh shadows or blown-out highlights.
* **Postprocessing:** `EffectComposer` with selective `Bloom` (subtle threshold to prevent distracting neon halos).

### 2.3 Camera Controller & Viewport Interactions
* **Dynamic Centering:** `THREE.Box3` automatically calculates the bounding box and centers the heart mesh precisely at coordinate `[0, 0, 0]`.
* **Smooth Lerp Transitions:** Camera transitions to target presets using delta-time lerping (`delta * 3.8`), releasing control seamlessly to `OrbitControls` once the transition reaches `<0.025` distance.
* **Control Presets Verified:**
  1. *Anterior View:* Camera at `[0, 0, 4.5]` — frames LAD and anterior wall.
  2. *Lateral View:* Camera at `[-3.8, 0.5, 2.2]` — frames LCX and obtuse marginal territory.
  3. *Inferior View:* Camera at `[0, -3.8, 2.4]` — frames diaphragmatic surface and distal RCA.
  4. *Right AV View:* Camera at `[3.6, 0.8, 2.4]` — frames proximal and mid RCA.
  5. *Overview:* Camera at `[0, 1.2, 5.0]` — balanced full-organ framing.

### 2.4 Visualization Boundaries & Clinical Caveats
* **Vessel Highlight vs. Plaque Localization:** The 3D scene highlights entire arterial branches proportionally to their model-estimated stenosis probability. It does **not** render fake intravascular plaques, stenotic narrowing, or synthetic calcifications.
* **Disclaimer Overlay:** A persistent floating note reminds users: *"Coronary vessel highlight denotes model-estimated territory stenosis probability, not physical intravascular plaque coordinates."*

## 3. UI/UX, Design Aesthetics & Accessibility Audit

### 3.1 Design System & Typography
* **Color Palette:** Curated clinical palette adhering to modern hospital software standards: Slate-50 background (`#F8FAFC`), crisp white card containers (`#FFFFFF`), slate-900 primary headers, and semantic accents (Emerald `#059669` for low risk, Amber `#D97706` for moderate risk, Crimson `#DC2626` for high risk, Rose `#F43F5E` for LAD, Teal `#0D9488` for LCX, Orange `#F97316` for RCA).
* **Typography:** Clean sans-serif hierarchy with monospaced numerical chips (`font-mono`) for biomarkers, probabilities, and SHAP delta values.

### 3.2 Responsive Behavior & Layout
* **Desktop (1280px+):** Generous 12-column grid layouts with balanced sidebars and sticky navigation.
* **Tablet & Mobile (375px–768px):** Clean single-column fallback with horizontal scroll chips for vessel selection and responsive canvas viewport height.

### 3.3 Accessibility (a11y) & Usability
* **Buttons & Controls:** Unique descriptive labels and `aria-label` attributes across all interactive buttons, scenario presets, and camera switches.
* **Contrast Ratios:** Text against white and light slate backgrounds exceeds WCAG AA 4.5:1 ratio.
* **State Clarity:** Distinct visual states for active, hovered, loading, and awaiting-assessment conditions.
